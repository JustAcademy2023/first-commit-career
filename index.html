The fresher's git log

Five commits that take you from empty folder to interview invite. Read from the bottom of your own story upward.

a1b2c3d

Pick a lane, then a language

Front end, back end or full stack: choose the kind of work you enjoy first. Then learn one language deeply instead of five badly.

e4f5a6b

Build three projects you can explain

One polished page, one full app with a database, one tool that fixes a problem you noticed. Deploy them and write a clear README.

c7d8e9f

Fix the resume, wake up the profile

One page, specific results, honest keywords. Make your headline and GitHub easy for a recruiter to understand in under a minute.

0a1b2c3

Apply weekly, follow up politely

Fifteen tailored applications beat two hundred copies. Track each one and learn from every reply, including the nos.

9f8e7d6

Talk through your thinking in the interview

Tell project stories, say "here is how I would find out" when stuck, and keep going. Merge to offer.
